About Me

Creating stories through Art and Design.

I am a visual artist and designer with a foundation in Fine Art from Sir J.J. School of Art and currently pursuing a degree in Design at Atlas SkillTech University. My practice spans watercolor, acrylic, and digital media, with a particular focus on watercolor.

Artist Statement
My work explores emotions, memories, and human experiences through realistic symbolism. Inspired by travel, landscapes, and everyday observations, I use visual storytelling to transform ordinary subjects into reflections on connection, change, and resilience. By combining observation with imagination, I create artworks that invite viewers to pause, reflect, and find their own meaning within each piece.
